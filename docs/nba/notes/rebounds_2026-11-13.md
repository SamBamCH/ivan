# Rebounds ran high in the World: the defence's share (2026-11-13)

**Finding.** The World credited a defender with every miss the offence did not rebound. In the box scores a player is
credited with 83-84% of them (2025-26: 42,163 player defensive rebounds of 50,093 such misses over 1,302 team-games,
0.842; 2026-27 so far 0.830 over 348; team-game 10-50-90: 0.74 / 0.83 / 0.91, counting half the missed free throws
as live, as the World does). The rest are team and dead-ball rebounds. That is 5 to 6 rebounds a team-game too many,
about 0.4 to 0.6 a rotation player, and it showed on the stat hover's chart (ours to the right of his games).

**Change** (`gamesim/world.py`). The defence gets a share `p_dreb`, built like its steal and block shares: the five
defenders' own defensive-rebound rates times minutes, over the misses the opponent is expected to leave (its attempts
scaled by its own attempt factor, half its missed free throws, less its own offensive rebounds), held between 0.6 and
0.97; 0.84 (`LEAGUE_DEFAULTS`, and `teams.json` from the next `gamesim.py fit-script`) when there is nothing to
divide by. Each minute's leftover misses are a defender's with that probability. So the drawn rebounds of a team are
its plan's (minutes x rates) in expectation, and still rise and fall with the misses actually drawn. Nothing else in
the draw changed.

**Backtest.** The as-of rerun's scorecard (`backtest.rerun`: inputs from the first tip record, history cut at the day)
for every held slate with a tip record, 22 slates from 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-12, 500 worlds, seed 0, the same inputs on
the code before and after; pooled over 2,448 player-games:

Rebounds' share of actuals inside the 10-90 band went from 0.783 to 0.792; the game checks (possessions, total,
margin) did not move. The rebound bias fell on 21 of the 22 slates.

**What is left.** The five most recent slates (11-08 to 11-12) still sit at +0.1 to +0.8 after the change (pooled
about +0.3), where the October slates sit at -0.2: the plan's own rebound rates run a little high lately, and on
11-10 the minutes did too (+1.7 a player). That is the mapping (rates and minutes), not the draw, and is the next
thing to look at if the chart still leans right.
