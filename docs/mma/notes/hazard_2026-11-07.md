# 2026-11-07: one risk of finish per fighter

The idea, from a brainstorm on slate day: every fighter has a rate of significant strikes and
takedowns and a risk of ending the fight; the longer the fight goes the more of a fighter's score
comes from production, and the less likely it is that the fight is still going. That is a
competing-risks survival model: a finish hazard per fighter, production rates per fighter, the fight
ends at the first stoppage, the survival curve decays, the final bell is the censoring point.

## What the markets say about the hazard's shape

The 2026-10-24 card's hand-entered fight markets (`fight_odds.prev.csv` at the time: u60 sec,
1st round, itd, one-sided American prices as posted) were checked against a **constant** hazard
fitted to the ITD price alone, over a 15-minute fight:

| check over 14 fights | market / flat-hazard prediction |
|---|---|
| round-1 finish probability | 1.05 |
| under-60-seconds probability | 1.43 |
| round-1 share of all finishes | 0.47 (what a flat hazard gives at these ITD levels) |

So the round-1 price carried no information the ITD price did not: "half of finishes come in round 1"
is a flat hazard applied to fights that finish 60 to 70% of the time, not a front-loaded one. The two
rows that looked off were the two five-rounders, run at 15 minutes in the check; at 25 minutes they
close. The first minute is the one deviation and most of it is vig on a 20-1 shot, so no shape is
fitted to it; `fight.first_minute_mult` exists for anyone who wants the bump (default 1).

Caveats: one card, 14 fights, one-sided vigged prices. No other card's hand markets survive (each
ingest of a new card moves the previous files to `.prev`, and the sample card under `tests/mma/data`
is an older event with no scores on disk), so a projected-vs-actual comparison of the two models was
not possible; the ufcstats results give the finish truth for future cards.

## The model (hazard.py)

Per fight: P(finish) from the fight's ITD price, or the two fighters' ITD prices summed (capped at
0.97), else 0.5. Λ = -ln(1 - P(finish)) / T. Per fighter: share of the stoppages from the two ITD
prices against each other (moneyline split when a side lacks one); decision share = (P(win) - share
× P(finish)) / (1 - P(finish)), so the simulated win rate is the moneyline. Finish time exponential,
cut at T; the round of the stoppage gives the DK bonus. Strikes and takedowns: a rate per second such
that the posted line is the mean of the simulated count (the loser's half-share is folded into the
divisor), a Gamma multiplier per simulation (`rate_cv` 0.25) and Poisson counts.

What it removes from the weekly hand entry: the fight u60 and round-1 prices and the fighter
round-1 price. What remains: one ITD price per fighter (the fight's ITD is optional), Win (from the
API), the strikes and takedowns lines. The next step is a prior for those from fighter history
(ufcstats: finishes and strikes per minute fought, times finished per minute), so a blank cell gets a
number instead of a default.

## Not done

No backtest against real scores was run before switching the default (the user's call: the logic is
sound, the markets agree on the shape). The scoring weights (`sig_strike` 0.8, loser share 0.5) were
fitted under the tree model's time exponent and carry over unchanged; the hazard model holds the
lines as means, which the tree model did not, so projections run higher than before on the same
inputs. The tree model stays selectable (`fight.model = tree`).
