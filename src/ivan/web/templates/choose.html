The chooser scores every candidate on quality (a weighted blend of its simulated cash % and ROI, each scaled 0–1 across the candidates), takes the best one, and then repeatedly takes the lineup with the highest quality + λ × distance from the lineups already picked. Distance is measured between the lineups' simulated point totals across all simulations: two lineups that boom and bust together are close, even if they share few players.

2 · The solution space

The same candidates laid out so that lineups whose simulated totals move together sit close ({{ (v.explained_variance[0] + v.explained_variance[1])|pct1 }} of the differences between lineups are captured by these two axes). Quality-only picks cluster in one region; the diversity term spreads the blue picks across the map so your entries don't all win and lose in the same simulations.

3 · The greedy walk

{{ v.walk_svg|safe }}

Each bar is one pick: the dark part is the lineup's quality, the light part is the diversity bonus (λ × its mean distance from the earlier picks) that won it the spot. The dashed line is the quality of the k-th best lineup on quality alone, so the gap down to the dark bar is what each pick gave up to be different. Raise λ and the light parts grow while the dark parts fall.
